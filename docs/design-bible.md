# Surf Mangalore — Design Bible

## 1. Brand Positioning

### Brand essence
Surf Mangalore is a premium surf experience brand that blends ocean adventure, hospitality, safety, lifestyle, and community. It should feel like a polished coastal escape, not a generic lesson provider.

### Brand promise
Every interaction should make visitors feel:
- invited into the ocean
- safe in the hands of experts
- part of a joyful coastal community
- ready for discovery and adventure

### Brand personality
- Confident
- Warm
- Elevated
- Playful
- Professional
- Ocean-native

### Voice
The voice should be clear, cinematic, encouraging, and lightly aspirational. It should feel like a premium travel brand with a real human heartbeat.

### Tone
Use a tone that is:
- calm but energetic
- polished but not stiff
- adventurous but reassuring
- expressive without being dramatic

### Emotional identity
The brand should communicate:
- Ocean
- Freedom
- Adventure
- Joy
- Community
- Safety
- Professionalism
- Discovery
- Fun

### Keywords
Premium, coastal, energetic, trustworthy, effortless, adventurous, welcoming, modern, refined, joyful.

### Things the brand should feel like
- A curated ocean escape
- A premium adventure experience
- A confident local guide to the coast
- A modern hospitality brand with soul

### Things the brand should avoid
- Cheap or overly touristy language
- Loud, gimmicky visuals
- Overly corporate messaging
- Generic surf clichés
- Childish or playful branding that undermines professionalism

## 2. Color Language

### Core design principle
The color system should feel premium, coastal, and atmospheric. It should be grounded in natural ocean tones and elevated through refined contrast and soft glass surfaces.

### Ocean primary colors
Use these as the primary identity colors:
- Teal aqua: #7AD6D1
- Ocean cyan: #4FC3B8
- Seafoam glow: #A8E7E1

### Deep ocean colors
Use for depth, sections, and strong contrast:
- Deep navy: #04131B
- Midnight blue: #071B28
- Coastal slate: #0F2A3D

### Sky colors
Use for airy, open, premium backgrounds:
- Soft sky: #DDEFF3
- Mist blue: #C7DCE3
- Pale horizon: #F2F7F8

### Sand colors
Use for warmth and texture:
- Sunlit sand: #EFD9B5
- Dune beige: #D6C0A1
- Warm coral sand: #C8AA86

### Natural accent colors
Use sparingly for emphasis and energy:
- Sunset orange: #FF8F4A
- Coral glow: #FF9F6A
- Sea salt gold: #F2C06B

### Background colors
Use these as base surfaces:
- Main background: #04131B
- Elevated surface: #081C28
- Strong surface: #112D3C
- Soft surface: #173447

### Text colors
- Primary text: #F4F8FB
- Secondary text: #AAC0CC
- Muted text: #6F8694

### CTA colors
- Primary CTA: teal aqua with strong contrast
- Secondary CTA: soft glass or translucent outline
- Urgent action: sunset orange only for high-priority calls to action

### Glass effects
Use transparent layers with soft blur and subtle borders:
- Glass base: rgba(255,255,255,0.08)
- Glass border: rgba(255,255,255,0.12)
- Glass shadow: rgba(4,19,27,0.24)

### Gradient rules
Use gradients rarely and intentionally. They should feel cinematic, not loud.
- Hero gradients: deep ocean to sunrise coral
- Surface gradients: teal to transparent blue
- Accent gradients: teal to orange only for emphasis

### Color usage rules
- Use ocean teal for energy, movement, and premium action states
- Use deep navy for structure, surfaces, and serious content areas
- Use sand and warm accents for comfort, hospitality, and human warmth
- Use coral sparingly for urgency and emotional pull
- Avoid bright neon palettes

## 3. Typography System

### Heading style
Use a refined, modern sans-serif with a slightly editorial feel. Headings should feel confident and premium, never overly playful.
Recommended family direction:
- Manrope, Inter, or a similar high-quality contemporary sans-serif

### Body style
Body copy should be clean, readable, and calm. It should feel premium but approachable.

### Button text style
Buttons should use medium weight and slightly tighter tracking for confidence and clarity.

### Numbers/statistics style
Numbers should feel crisp and modern. Use a slightly bolder weight and strong spacing for impact.

### Navigation style
Navigation should feel understated, simple, and confident. It should not compete with the hero.

### Letter spacing rules
- Headings: tight, but not cramped
- Buttons: slightly expanded
- Badges and labels: elegant uppercase tracking
- Body copy: normal

### Font weight rules
- Display headings: 700
- Section headings: 600
- Subheadings: 600
- Body: 400–500
- Buttons: 500–600

### Text hierarchy rules
- H1: strongest, heroic, editorial
- H2: confident and structured
- H3: clear and concise
- Body: relaxed and legible
- Captions and labels: understated and precise

### Typography rhythm
Use generous line-height for body content and tighter line-height for short headlines. Avoid text blocks that feel dense or overly academic.

## 4. Image Direction

### Photography philosophy
Images should feel cinematic, premium, and natural. They should celebrate the ocean without looking like a stock photo library.

### Subject
Use:
- surfers in motion
- coastal landscapes
- horizon lines
- sunrise and sunset scenes
- real people enjoying the experience
- hands-on coaching moments
- scenic travel moments

### Composition
Prefer:
- strong horizon lines
- asymmetrical framing
- natural depth
- environmental context
- minimal clutter
- a sense of movement

### Lighting
Prefer:
- golden hour
- soft early morning light
- misty coastal atmosphere
- dramatic but natural contrast

### Colors
Images should feel:
- oceanic
- warm
- luminous
- clean
- cinematic

### Camera angle
Use:
- low-angle perspectives for energy
- wide landscape framing for scale
- intimate close-ups for emotion
- subtle motion blur for movement

### People photography
People should feel:
- alive
- joyful
- confident
- relaxed
- real

### Ocean photography
Ocean imagery should feel powerful but not aggressive. It should show beauty, depth, and motion.

### Adventure shots
Adventure images should feel active and elevated, with a polished travel aesthetic.

### What images should never be used
- Generic beach snapshots
- Overexposed stock surf clichés
- Loud party-style imagery
- Low-quality smartphone photos
- Cartoonish, over-saturated, or overly dramatic visuals
- Images that feel unsafe, messy, or chaotic

## 5. UI Components Language

### Buttons
- Rounded-pill shape for emphasis
- Soft depth and subtle lift on hover
- Primary button should feel immediate and confident
- Outline buttons should feel elegant and premium
- Avoid harsh borders or boxy shapes

### Cards
- Rounded corners with soft depth
- Use glass and layered surfaces
- Border should be subtle and refined
- Avoid overly heavy shadows

### Forms
- Clean spacing
- Soft borders
- Clear labels
- Premium but minimal styling
- Fields should feel calm and easy to complete

### Badges
- Compact, modern, and understated
- Use soft background treatments and low visual weight
- Great for proof points and trust elements

### Icons
- Simple, line-led, modern icons
- Should feel integrated, not decorative for decoration’s sake
- Prefer soft contrast over saturated iconography

### Images
- Use rounded edges selectively
- Layer with subtle overlays and depth
- Never use images without strong framing or composition

### Sections
- Use generous padding
- Layer visual atmosphere behind content
- Maintain a calm rhythm with clear section boundaries

### Containers
- Max width should be generous but not stretched
- Use clear content breathing room
- Maintain strong alignment and spacing consistency

### Dividers
- Use soft lines, gradients, or transparent borders
- Avoid heavy separators that feel rigid

## 6. Animation Language

### Animation philosophy
Animation should feel smooth, intentional, and premium. It should support the storytelling rather than distract from it.

### When animations should happen
- On CTA hover
- On card reveal
- On page transitions
- On section entrance when appropriate
- On subtle motion cues in hero imagery

### When animations should NOT happen
- On every interaction
- On repeated decorative elements
- In a way that feels flashy, noisy, or gimmicky
- In a way that weakens clarity or usability

### Duration
- Hover transitions: 160–220ms
- Entrance motion: 300–500ms
- Page transitions: 400–600ms

### Easing
Use smooth and natural easing such as cubic-bezier curves rather than bounce-based motion.

### Hover effects
- slight lift
- soft glow
- subtle color shift
- gentle border highlight

### Scroll effects
Use restrained reveals and subtle parallax only when it adds elegance. Avoid excessive movement.

### Page transitions
Use soft fade or subtle slide transitions with a calm pace. Prioritize elegance and continuity.

## 7. Layout Rules

### Container width
Use generous content width with strong breathing room:
- Small container: 40rem
- Medium container: 64rem
- Large container: 80rem

### Spacing rhythm
Use a consistent spacing system based on an 8px rhythm. Keep spacing generous and intentional.

### Section padding
- Mobile: 24px–40px
- Tablet: 48px–72px
- Desktop: 72px–120px

### Grid system
Use a flexible grid that supports:
- single-column mobile
- two-column tablet
- richer asymmetric desktop layouts

### Desktop rules
Use more layered composition, overlap, and visual depth. Hero sections should feel editorial and cinematic.

### Tablet rules
Maintain clarity and avoid crowding. Reduce decorative overlap while keeping premium spacing.

### Mobile rules
Prioritize readability and tap targets. The mobile experience must still feel premium, not like a compressed desktop version.

### Whitespace philosophy
Whitespace is part of the design language. It should feel luxurious, calm, and deliberate. Never fill every area with content.

## 8. Surf Inspired Elements

### Wave language
Use wave-inspired forms subtly and elegantly. They should support the layout, not dominate it.

### Curves
Soft curves can be used in backgrounds, section edges, and decorative surfaces.

### Water effects
Use translucent overlays, gradient depth, and subtle motion ideas to suggest ocean motion.

### Organic shapes
Organic eroded shapes can be used for decorative emphasis, but they should remain refined.

### Gradients
Use gradients to suggest horizon, sunrise, foam, and depth.

### Textures
Textures should be soft and atmospheric. Avoid noisy grain or childish patterning.

### Design rule
Surf-inspired elements should feel sophisticated and premium, never cartoonish or theme-park-like.

---

## 9. Conversion Rules

### CTA placement
Primary CTA should be visible near the hero and repeated in the right places without feeling aggressive.

### Booking prompts
Use clear, calm booking language. Make the next step feel easy and trustworthy.

### Trust signals
Show social proof, safety, coaching credentials, and guest confidence. These should feel integrated, not forced.

### Social proof placement
Place social proof where hesitation is highest: after experience explanation and before conversion.

### Visitor journey
The page should guide the visitor through:
1. Curiosity
2. Excitement
3. Trust
4. Confidence
5. Booking

The experience should feel emotionally guided and carefully paced.

---

## 10. System Extensibility

This design system must support:
- Homepage
- Lessons
- Events
- Gallery
- Booking
- Admin Dashboard

### Design system principle
Every screen should feel like it belongs to the same visual family, even if the layout changes. The system should be modular and reusable.

### Reuse rules
- Reuse color roles and spacing logic
- Reuse component styling patterns
- Use consistent internal hierarchy and motion language
- Let each page express the same brand DNA, but adapt the composition to the purpose

---

## 11. Production Notes

### Accessibility
- Ensure sufficient contrast for all text
- Keep interactive states clearly visible
- Maintain comfortable tap targets
- Preserve readability across all screen sizes

### Performance
- Favor optimized imagery and cautious use of effects
- Avoid visual overload
- Keep motion subtle and purposeful

### Maintainability
- Design tokens should be the source of truth
- Components should inherit from token-based styles
- Avoid ad hoc one-off styles whenever possible

---

## Final Design Direction Summary
Surf Mangalore should feel like a premium ocean experience brand that blends adventure, hospitality, safety, and joy. The visual language should be refined, calm, cinematic, and modern. It should feel elevated without becoming cold, and adventurous without becoming chaotic.
